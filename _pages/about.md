I am an applied microeconomist working on food demand and nutrition. Using
household scanner data at the transaction level, linked to nutrition information
for the products purchased, I study how large shifts in what people eat change
the composition and healthfulness of what households buy. Work in progress asks
how food manufacturers respond to shifts like this, through product mix and
reformulation, and what they imply for demand further up the chain.

**Job market paper:** _"The Effects of GLP-1 Medications on Household Food
Demand"_ (with Joseph Balagtas). Among the first evidence on how GLP-1
medications change food demand and its nutritional content, built from the
Nutrition Facts of every item purchased: spending falls more than calories, and
the basket shrinks without getting healthier. _In preparation._
